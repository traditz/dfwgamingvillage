The Strogg are artifact creatures, so the whole set is artifacts-matter in Jeskai colours: blue for Strogg tech and drones, red for soldiers, Tanks and explosives, white for Medics (they really do resurrect Strogg), armour and force fields. Weapon ladder Blaster → BFG10K, Unit duals that enter untapped when you hold two artifacts, and the Jorg → Makron boss fight. Mission-pack cards are tagged [TR] The Reckoning / [GZ] Ground Zero.
