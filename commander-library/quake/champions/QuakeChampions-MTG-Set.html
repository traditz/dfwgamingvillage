A roster, not a campaign. Sixteen Champions — a custom creature subtype, every one of them legendary, because the arena admits one of each — pulled out of every world and every century by the Vadrigar. Each arrives with a passive trait and one signature Active ability that can be used only once a turn. Jund colours: red is the fighters and the violence, black is the Dreamlands and the Elder Gods who run the place, green is flesh and armour. Weapons are colourless Equipment, because in the arena a gun belongs to whoever picked it up last. Unofficial fan content.
