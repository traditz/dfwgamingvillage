Wording & feel checks

Oracle templating lint + EDHREC salt cross-reference.

Templating. 10 of 898 rules sentences have no close printed-Oracle analog — all reviewed as intentional custom-mechanic composites; two genuine wording misses were fixed.

The 10 accepted flags
  • Joker, Reckoning Marine (quake2, 0.50)
    “Whenever ~ attacks, Equipment attached to it gain "Equipped creature has menace" until end of turn.”
  • Big Gun Barrage (quake2, 0.55)
    “If no creature with flying was dealt damage this way, it deals 3 damage to any target instead.”
  • Commander's Head (quake2, 0.60)
    “{3}, {T}, Sacrifice ~: Gain control of target artifact creature.”
  • Harvester (quake3, 0.52)
    “{2}, {T}, Remove three skull counters from ~: Create a 2/2 black Skeleton Warrior creature token with haste.”
  • Capture the Flag (quake3, 0.57)
    “At the beginning of your end step, if there are three or more flag counters on ~, remove them and draw two cards.”
  • Nailgun (quake3, 0.61)
    “Equipped creature gets +1/+1 and has "{T}: ~ deals 1 damage to each of up to two target creatures."”
  • Recon (quake4, 0.58)
    “If a creature died this turn, draw two cards instead.”
  • The Lurching King (champions, 0.54)
    “Whenever ~ attacks, defending player sacrifices a creature unless they discard a card.”
  • Tri-Bolt (champions, 0.57)
    “Whenever equipped creature attacks, it deals 2 damage divided as you choose among one or two target creatures defending player controls.”
  • Ghost Walk (champions, 0.57)
    “Target creature you control phases out.”
⚠
Salt watch. Pentagram of Protection and both Invulnerabilities are analogs of Teferi's Protection, a top-of-salt-list card on EDHREC. They are the likeliest source of 'balance' complaints being about feel rather than power. Kept deliberately — they are iconic Quake powerups — but they are the first cards to revisit if the table keeps grumbling. Re-check any time with python common/sim/run.py --salt

  1. Copy quake/balance/playtests/TEMPLATE.md to a new file (one per game) and fill in winner, ending round, MVP and dead cards.
  2. Follow quake/balance/playtests/SCHEDULE.md so table time covers all 15 pod combinations.
  3. Run python common/sim/run.py --ingest-playtests, then python common/sim/balance_dashboard.py --collection quake — the comparison table appears here.

How honest are these numbers? The engine models mana, the curve, combat with keywords, removal, wipes, tokens, equipment, Sagas, commander tax/damage and every ability it could parse — but not the stack, counterspells or politics. Absolute win rates are not Magic; distances from baseline and side-by-side comparisons are the signal. Full methodology: quake/balance/BALANCE_PASS.md · raw numbers: quake/balance/report.md.

Regenerate after any sim run or playtest ingest: python common/sim/balance_dashboard.py
