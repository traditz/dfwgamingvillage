How honest are these numbers? The engine models mana, the curve, combat with keywords, removal, wipes, tokens, equipment, Sagas, commander tax/damage and every ability it could parse — but not the stack, counterspells or politics. Absolute win rates are not Magic; distances from baseline and side-by-side comparisons are the signal. Full methodology: doom/balance/BALANCE_PASS.md · raw numbers: doom/balance/report.md.
