Marvel Champions

Hero Decks & Villain Builder

Every hero ships with a community-tuned deck. Together, all the decks fit one copy of the whole collection. Then pick a villain (or two leaders for a Civil War VS game), tune the encounter deck and head to the table.

Heroes Villains Game Night Collection Rules & Setup

Deck lists are built from every published MarvelCDB decklist (recent decks count more) and solved so that all decks fit one copy of every product except the Cyclops and Phoenix hero packs. Scenario setups follow the official scenario inserts; rules follow the Rules Reference v1.8, and competitive (VS) mode the Civil War rulebook.
