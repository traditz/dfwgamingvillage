Search the Rulebooks

Full-text search of the rulebooks for the sets selected above, the Quick Reference Guide and the fan “Frequently Overlooked Rules” sheet. Rise of Fenris pages stay hidden until their spoiler gate is open (its spoiler-free opening pages, pp.2–5, are always searchable).

Setup and reference content grounded in the official Scythe rulebook (2022 printing), the Quick Reference Guide, and the Invaders from Afar, The Wind Gambit, The Rise of Fenris and Modular Board rulebooks; a fan-made “Frequently Overlooked Rules” sheet is used only as a checklist. Page numbers are printed page numbers, except the Quick Reference Guide, the Modular Board rules and the fan Overlooked Rules sheet, which are cited by PDF page. The Automa (solo) rulebook is not covered.
